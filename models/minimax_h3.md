# Model adapter · MiniMax H3

Camera text for MiniMax H3 (T2VA, I2VA, FL2VA, L2VA, Ref2VA). This adapter is the ONE H3 camera core (PROJECT_GOAL.md): it writes **only the camera sentences** for a `[Shot N]`, the same sentences for every mode. The prompt format belongs to MiniMax's `h3-prompt-writing` guide (SRC-008 = LOCAL-001); `scripts/h3_wrappers.py` places the camera core into that format for each mode (T2VA / I2VA / FL2VA / L2VA → Base structure, Ref2VA → Full-reference structure) and never writes camera language of its own.

## Four layers (PROJECT_GOAL.md)
Every movement is compiled into four labelled layers; the flat camera text is their sentences in this order. `render … --layers` prints them with their sources; `--json` carries them as `h3` per shot.

| Layer | Source | What it holds | `/MS /PAN:R:45` |
|---|---|---|---|
| `camera_core` | MINIMAX_OFFICIAL (SRC-008 base-en 4.3) | the official motion vocabulary; `with small/large amplitude` and `at slow/fast speed` only when the DSL gives them | The camera pans right. |
| `temporal_clarifier` | PROJECT_DEFINED, evidence id attached | how the move spreads over the clip | — (a push: The push continues steadily over the whole video.) |
| `derived_visual_constraint` | PROJECT_DEFINED, evidence id attached | what the move does to the picture: end framing, what stays in frame, amounts in degrees / percent / metres that the official vocabulary cannot carry | The pan turns about 45 degrees. |
| `negative_clarifier` | PROJECT_DEFINED (research/07 D39) | what the camera does not do; omitted when a simultaneous move contradicts it | The camera stays in place. |

Fidelity (PROJECT_GOAL rule 1): a user-given amount, direction or speed is never dropped and never silently added. `/PAN:R:45`, `/TILT:UP:30`, `/ROLL:CW:15` keep their degrees as a derived constraint; `/DOLLYIN:SLOW` writes `at slow speed`; `/ORBIT:R:FULL` gets no amplitude or speed token unless the DSL asks for one (`/ORBIT:R:FULL:FAST`). The crash zoom's `with large amplitude at fast speed` comes from the command's own definition (a sudden, very fast zoom), not from the adapter; the render metadata reports such values as `DEFINITION_IMPLIED` (crash zoom speed FAST and amount LARGE, whip pan speed FAST), never as `UNSPECIFIED` (CL-031).

**The sentence split matters (H3LAB-PAN-NEGATIVE-CLARIFIER-01, CL-036).** In one measured scope (PAN:R, I2VA, medium-shot first frame, `LOCAL_H3_I2VA_PDD8_Q_416`) the standalone negative clarifier "The camera stays in place." suppressed the pan in 3/3 seeds, while the same idea inside one sentence ("stays in place and pans right") did not; the core alone restored the motion but still not a real pan (0/3). The finding is recorded with its scope in the profile (`lab_findings`), and the adapter output is unchanged (`adapter_action.change_output: false`): leaving the sentence out restores motion, not the camera semantics. `h3_wrappers.py --layers camera_core` remains available to a caller who wants the core alone.

## Output rules
1. **English, plain, one idea per sentence, ≤35 words** (LOCAL-002 H3LAB-PLAIN-01). The adapter warns on longer sentences.
2. **Order** (SRC-005 SKILL:43-45): framing → lens → height → angle → focus → steadiness → movement → gaze/continuity.
3. **Push / pull:** opening "The camera starts on a <size> that frames …", core "The camera pushes in[ at slow speed][ toward {SUBJECT}].", temporal clarifier "The push continues steadily over the whole video." (omitted for FAST, BRISK and large amplitude), derived constraint "The final frame is a <size> of {SUBJECT} …", negative clarifier "The focal length stays the same." (LOCAL-002 H3LAB-PUSH-01: start → end framing is what H3 followed). No `with small amplitude` unless asked (inert, SRC-009). If no end size is given, the adapter warns instead of inventing one.
3b. **Speed:** SLOW/VSLOW/IMPERCEPTIBLE → `at slow speed`, FAST → `at fast speed` (the documented tokens, SRC-008 base-en:114-115; LOCAL-002 H3LAB-PUSH-02 measured speed words as weak, so do not rely on them). BRISK has no H3 token: it is written as normal speed, with a warning. The adapter never adds a speed the user did not give (PROJECT_GOAL fidelity rule 1). The crash zoom is the one move that carries `at fast speed` on its own, because the command is defined as a sudden, very fast zoom (registry); the full orbit no longer does (rule 4).
3c. **No self-contradiction:** "stays in place", "does not turn", "staying level" and "the focal length stays the same" are written only when no simultaneous move breaks them (research/07 D39).
3d. **Sequences:** time windows use the lab's verified time phrasing (LOCAL-002 H3LAB-TIME-01): "For the first N seconds, …" then "At about the N-second mark, …". A later step never restates the opening framing and never says "over the whole video"; the last step of a push says "for the rest of the video" (research/07 D41). Whether H3 keeps the order was measured in the sequence cells of the reality matrix.
3e. **Plain English, checked by the lab lint:** no similes or literary words. Bird's-eye is "the camera is far above and looks straight down. The ground fills the frame, and {SUBJECT} is small in it", and the shoulder rig is "sways slightly, carried on a shoulder". When the lab is on this machine, `scripts/audit.py` runs the lab lint's sentence check on every H3 render (research/07 D41).
4. **Full orbit:** core "The camera circles {SUBJECT} all the way around in an arc shot, ending back at the front." `at fast speed` is written only when the DSL asks for it (`/ORBIT:R:FULL:FAST`); no amplitude word is added. SRC-009 completed the circle only "with large amplitude at fast speed" (orbit-360: 72 → 203 px/step), so a FULL orbit without :FAST gets a warning instead of silent tokens (PROJECT_GOAL fidelity rule 1; up to CL-029 the adapter added them). Partial arcs: "The camera moves in an arc shot around {SUBJECT} …" with the degrees as a derived constraint.
5. **Static:** "The camera holds a Static Shot throughout." (LOCAL-002 H3LAB-STATIC-01).
6. **Tracking:** "follows … in a tracking shot, keeping …'s <body part> inside the frame the entire way" (LOCAL-002 H3LAB-TRACK-01).
7. **Never name a visible rig** (gimbal, steadicam, drone, slider): describe the effect (SRC-009 gear.md: a named body rig was drawn in frame).
8. **Whip pan** names where it lands and that the camera stays on it to the end (SRC-009 whip-pan); do not ask for motion blur.
9. **Dutch:** static state sentence; if the cant does not render, SRC-009 reached it by describing a counterclockwise roll that stops at the tilted angle and holds.
10. **Rack focus:** "The focus then racks from A to B …" — verified with a static camera and two depth planes (SRC-009 rack-focus).
11. **No bracket commands** like `[Push in]` — that is older Hailuo syntax, not H3 (research/04 section 2).
12. **Placement:** `python scripts/h3_wrappers.py <t2va|i2va|fl2va|l2va|ref2va> "<dsl>" [content.json]` puts the sentences in `[Shot N]` of `integrated_multimodal_description` (Base modes, with the official alignment line for I2VA / FL2VA / L2VA) or `detailed_description` (Ref2VA: the six fields in order, `<Subject N>` labels), right after the opening style/composition sentence (SRC-008 base-en:78). `--layers camera_core,negative_clarifier` keeps only the chosen layers; `--lint` runs the lab lint when it is on this machine (LOCAL-002). Tests: tests/h3_wrappers.md.
13. **Truck left, alone in a shot:** core "The camera trucks left.", derived constraint "In the final frame {SUBJECT} is on the right side of the frame.", negative clarifier "The camera slides sideways and does not turn." Without the final-frame clause H3 sometimes locked the subject in the centre and slid only the background (LOCAL-002 H3LAB-TRUCK-01, 3/3 with H3-PDD 8 steps). Truck right keeps the plain wording: the mirrored clause was 1/3 (H3LAB-TRUCK-02). Pan left keeps the plain wording: no tested wording reached 3/3 (H3LAB-PAN-01). Inside a combination or a sequence the clause is not written, because it was verified only alone. The 3/3 was measured with the pre-refactor single sentence ("The camera trucks left, sliding sideways, so that in the final frame {SUBJECT} is on the right side of the frame."); the layered three-sentence form has not been re-validated (`PRE_REFACTOR_WORDING`, CL-036).

## Warnings the adapter raises
- no end size on a push/pull; nothing near the lens (a push then reads as a zoom — the DSL cannot add objects);
- lens/gear words are cosmetic on H3;
- untested direction in SRC-009 (Pan Left, Tilt Up, Pedestal Down, Roll CW, Truck Right, and Truck Left inside a combination or a sequence — SRC-009 direction.md); a Truck Left alone uses the locally verified wording (rule 13) and gets no warning;
- sequences / multi-shot: H3 cannot place events at exact seconds; one shot per generation.

## H3 Camera Production Evidence (v1.0)
There is one camera DSL and one evidence system. Every measured H3 result belongs to the **H3 Camera Production Evidence** and is one of two types:

| Evidence type | What it verifies | Stored as (`subject_context`) | Routing |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CONSTRAINED_PRODUCTION** | camera motion together with production constraints: start and end shot size, landing, screen position, behaviour relative to a reference target | `CHARACTER_ANCHORED` (the default context) | default, or `--h3-subject-context constrained_production` |
| **CAMERA_ONLY** | camera motion itself (STATIC, PAN, TILT, TRUCK, PEDESTAL, DOLLY, ORBIT around a fixed landmark), measured on a fixed set with nothing to frame | `ENVIRONMENT_ONLY` | `--h3-subject-context camera_only` |

The reference target is a test fixture: the CONSTRAINED_PRODUCTION tests used a person, the CAMERA_ONLY tests a corridor with a bench as the fixed landmark. It could be a group, a vehicle, an object or a structure; it is not a category of the DSL, and no grade is keyed by it. The stored names `character_anchored` and `environment_only` stay valid for backward compatibility; the routing block prints the stored names.

**Every grade is exact-scope.** It holds for its own mode + generation profile + command + direction + start framing + end framing (and end size) + angle + evidence type, nothing wider. A request outside a measured scope is UNVERIFIED; the routing may name related evidence, but never lends its grade. Grades: PRODUCTION_VALIDATED = 3 of 3 seeds PASS in that scope, CONDITIONAL = 2 of 3, LOW = 0 or 1 of 3, INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE = the input could not express the move or the validator could not measure enough to decide, UNVERIFIED = nothing was measured in that scope.
- **LOW is not a DSL error.** It says H3 did not reliably follow that camera text in that scope. The camera semantics are never changed to fit a model limitation; limitations are reported as warnings and routing notes.
- **INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE is not a model failure.** It records an input limitation or a measurement gap.
- **FL2VA is endpoint-conditioned.** It joins two given keyframes, so a camera move is only expressed when the last keyframe already shows its end. With the same picture as both keyframes the camera does not move (CAMERA_ONLY: 11 of 12 FL2VA routes INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE).

**Status.** `h3_production_baseline_status = COMPLETE`: the production baseline defined for v1.0 is complete (CONSTRAINED_PRODUCTION: the frozen I2VA baseline and the multi-mode baseline; CAMERA_ONLY: the free camera baseline; all five modes). `h3_full_command_space_validation = PARTIAL`, by design: not every canonical command x mode x profile x direction x shot size x landing x angle is measured, and nothing is generated to complete such a list; anything outside a measured scope stays UNVERIFIED and is validated when a production needs it. The 42-cell Ref2VA matrix below is a historical record (FINAL PARTIAL), kept unchanged.

Failure types seen so far (observed; causes not isolated):
- **Camera mechanism substitution:** a translation comes out as a rotation (truck right became a pan right; the same happened at the 20-step recipe and with an explicit near/mid/far depth sentence, H3LAB-TRUCK-02, H3LAB-TRUCK-03, H3LAB-RTRUCK-DEPTH-01). The cause is not attributable to the acceleration, a missing final-frame position or a missing foreground depth cue; the adapter adds no more wording for it.
- **Subject compensation / auto-reframe:** the subject stays in the centre while only the background slides (the truck-left clause fixed this case, H3LAB-TRUCK-01).
- **Temporal execution:** the direction is right, but the move starts late or the subject lags the background (pan left, H3LAB-PAN-01).
- **Framing drift:** the camera move is right but the frame is wider than asked (a medium shot comes out full body; pulls overshoot). A crop-line sentence for the first frame did not fix it (H3LAB-FRAMING-ANCHOR-01), a waist-cropped outfit reference did not fix it and broke the lower-body costume (H3LAB-FRAMING-REFERENCE-01), and a storyboard composition <Picture> did not fix it either (H3LAB-FRAMING-COMPOSITION-ANCHOR-01). Keep the full-body references. Detailed environment descriptions widen the frame: removing three environment-view sentences made the subject 2.4–3x larger in 2/3 seeds, though still not waist-up (H3LAB-FRAMING-SCENE-01, VERIFIED effect). The reference image size (`max` vs the production `match`) is not the cause (H3LAB-FRAMING-REFSIZE-01, 0/3 either way). The pipeline was audited end to end and cleared: the prompts reached the model word for word.

## Capability profile and production routing
`models/minimax_h3_profile.yaml` holds three things (CL-032): **generation_profiles** (`LOCAL_H3_REF2VA_PDD8_Q_416`, `LOCAL_H3_I2VA_PDD8_Q_416`, `LOCAL_H3_T2VA_PDD8_Q_416`, `LOCAL_H3_FL2VA_PDD8_Q_416`, `LOCAL_H3_L2VA_PDD8_Q_416`, `LOCAL_H3_PRODUCTION_REF2VA`, `LOCAL_H3_PRODUCTION_I2VA`, `OFFICIAL_REFERENCE` — one profile is one mode, and a local profile names its mode in its id; each records mode, checkpoint, text encoder, accelerator, steps, sampler, cfg, shift, resolution, frames, reference configuration, ref_image_size; a generation profile is an identity of the setup, never a grade and never an "H3 default"), **reality_evidence** keyed by profile (what was measured, not what is documented: text-only shot size LOW; environment descriptions widen the frame (VERIFIED); a strict medium or closer shot should start from a real first frame; five evidence tables — `viewpoint`, `focus`, `lens`, `continuity`, `camera_motion` — with the corrected H3_RELIABILITY grade of every matrix cell, each row carrying its profile and sitting in the table of what the cell tests, decided by the registry category of its commands and never by the name (/WIDEANGLE is LENS: a lens, not a shot size; /RACKFOCUS is FOCUS; the axis/eyeline storyboard cells are CONTINUITY; CL-033); seed sensitivity HIGH; reference image size not a root cause; the I2VA profile holds the first-frame framing evidence and the three production shots as PROVISIONAL records), and **production_routes** (each route bound to a mode + profile). The lookup needs the scope: `--h3-mode` and `--h3-profile <id>` on the CLI (the profile alone fixes the mode), or the wrapper's mode plus `--profile`:
- the first line names the evidence scope and its status (MEASURED, PROVISIONAL or UNVERIFIED);
- under the Ref2VA matrix profile: shot size tighter than a full shot (MFS, COWBOY, MS, MCU, CU, ECU) "reliability LOW — if the framing must be exact, use a real first frame (I2VA); if Ref2VA is kept, describe what environment the subject is in, not which environment elements must be visible; a waist-up frame is not guaranteed"; each viewpoint, focus, lens, continuity and camera-move row with its measured grade, but only for the shot size (and the end size, for a push, pull or zoom) the cell was measured with: `/MS /PAN:R` gets the pan-right row, `/FS /PAN:R` does not — the block then says "no row measured under this scope for start framing FS — UNVERIFIED" and names the row as related evidence with its grade ("related evidence (not applied): /MS /PAN:R = B (historical, H3R-PAN-R)") (a warning when the grade is C, D or F; when two cells tested the same commands, both rows are shown); seed sensitivity: judge the first render, try another seed before rewording;
- under the I2VA profile: the first-frame framing evidence (PROVISIONAL) and the production routes of DOLLYIN, DOLLYOUT, PAN, TRACKSIDE, FOLLOW, LEAD, TRUCK, TILT, PEDESTAL, ZOOMIN, ZOOMOUT and ORBIT, every line showing its own status (PROVISIONAL or POST_REFACTOR_VALIDATION); the evidence scope of a production route is mode + generation profile + direction + start framing + end framing + angle: a route is applied only to a move of the same direction, the same start framing, the same kind of end framing (`end_framing` FREE / SPECIFIED), the same end size and, when the route was measured with a stated angle, exactly that angle, so `/MS /DOLLYIN` and `/MS /DOLLYIN:MS>MCU` get different DOLLYIN routes, and `/MS /DOLLYIN:MS>CU`, `/FS /DOLLYIN:FS>MS` or `/FS /TRACKSIDE:R` get none: the block says "no production route measured under this scope for MS>CU — UNVERIFIED" and names the measured DSL of the related routes ("related evidence (not applied)") without their grade; the ORBIT route was measured for an arc of 45 degrees toward camera-right and carries `angle: 45` (CL-048): `/MS /ORBIT:R:45` gets it, while `/MS /ORBIT:R:90`, `/MS /ORBIT:R:FULL` and `/MS /ORBIT:R` without an angle get none ("no production route measured under this scope for start framing MS, angle 90 degrees — UNVERIFIED", with `/MS /ORBIT:R:45` named as related evidence) and `/MS /ORBIT:L:45` is answered with "measured under this scope for direction R only";
- with `--h3-subject-context camera_only` (stored name `environment_only`; CL-051, CL-052): the CAMERA_ONLY block of that profile only (no CONSTRAINED_PRODUCTION record, no matrix row), with the same scope rules: a CAMERA_ONLY route has no shot size (`start_framing: NONE`), so `/PAN:R` gets it and `/MS /PAN:R` does not; a locked shot (`/STATIC`) has routes of its own. Without the option (or with `constrained_production`, stored name `character_anchored`) the evidence type is CONSTRAINED_PRODUCTION, every earlier answer is unchanged, and a command that a profile measured only as CAMERA_ONLY says so ("measured under this profile for subject context ENVIRONMENT_ONLY only — not applied"). The other-scope list names the ENVIRONMENT_ONLY scopes after the others; an FL2VA CAMERA_ONLY route shows its input limitation (both keyframes are the same picture);
- under the T2VA, FL2VA and L2VA baseline profiles and, next to the matrix rows, under the Ref2VA profile: the sixteen multi-mode baseline routes (CL-050), with the same scope rules. T2VA also shows its own text-only shot-size record (LOW) with advice that names T2VA; the Ref2VA environment advice and its evidence are not applied to it. FL2VA shows "shot size …: from the first frame — reliability HIGH (POST_REFACTOR_VALIDATION)". L2VA has no general shot-size record: the shot size is UNVERIFIED there and each route carries its own start result;
- anything the asked scope has no evidence for is listed as **UNVERIFIED**: a move outside the baseline routes of a mode, the production profiles, an unknown profile, or a profile of another mode; with no profile given, every mode is UNVERIFIED. No grade is borrowed from another mode or profile; the block only points to where other evidence exists. There is no default profile: when none is given the block ends with "Specify --h3-profile to query measured evidence." and nothing is guessed. With `--lang zh` the sentences are Chinese while ids and enums (UNVERIFIED, PROVISIONAL, profile ids, grades) stay as they are.

**Scene rule for medium and closer shots (H3LAB-FRAMING-SCENE-01):** the scene text says *where the subject is* ("she stands in a stone courtyard"), never *what the frame must show* (two rows of pillars, the whole far wall, the gate, a big sky, a vast courtyard). Those sentences pushed the subject to 0.3–0.4 frame heights. The DSL does not write scene text; this rule is for the prompt around it.

The routing changes no camera text and no command: it says how far H3 was measured to follow the text, and which input to use when the framing matters.

## Before you generate (SRC-009 settings.md)
4-step turbo sampling suppresses camera motion almost entirely; a background reference plate halves the move; in two tests a reference video did not pass its camera motion on. Check these before blaming the prompt.

## Examples (actual adapter output)
`/MS /LOWANGLE /DOLLYIN:MS>MCU:SLOW`
> The camera looks up at {SUBJECT} from a low angle. The camera starts on a medium shot that frames {SUBJECT} from the waist up. The camera pushes in at slow speed. The push continues steadily over the whole video. The final frame is a medium close-up of {SUBJECT} from the chest up. The focal length stays the same.

`/FS /GROUNDLEVEL /TRACKSIDE:R /GIMBAL` (spec section 23)
> A full shot frames {SUBJECT} from head to toe. The camera is at ground level, just above the floor. The camera moves very smoothly and level, with no shake. The camera travels beside {SUBJECT} in a tracking shot, moving toward frame-right at {SUBJECT}'s pace. The camera stays at {SUBJECT}'s side the whole time, and {SUBJECT}'s whole body, feet included, stays inside the frame. {SUBJECT} stays about the same size while the background slides past behind.
